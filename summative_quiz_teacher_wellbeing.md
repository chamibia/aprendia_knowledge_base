### Q3–Q4 Bank: Understanding Questions (Q3 and Q4 are UNDERSTANDING — not Application)

* What is an example of a micro-wellbeing moment that could help a teacher?
  * Acceptable answers may reflect: short breathing pauses, stretching, drinking water, a quick movement break, stepping outside briefly, taking a few quiet minutes
  * *Keywords (do not reveal): pause, breathe, stretch, water, break, moment, reset, short, movement*

* Why is it important for teachers to recognize their own stressors instead of ignoring them?
  * Acceptable answers may reflect: self-awareness, emotional regulation, preventing burnout, protecting wellbeing, managing stress before it escalates
  * *Keywords (do not reveal): aware, recognize, regulate, burnout, protect, stress, manage, prevent, escalate*

* What is a "mindset reframe" and how can it help a teacher?
  * Acceptable answers may reflect: changing negative thinking into a more positive or growth-oriented view, focusing on what one can control, seeing challenges as temporary, building resilience and hope
  * *Keywords (do not reveal): reframe, mindset, positive, growth, control, temporary, resilience, shift, thinking*

### Q5–Q6 Bank: Application Questions (Q5 and Q6 are BOTH APPLICATION — not Understanding)

* A student is trying to explain a problem to you, but you are already thinking about the next step of the lesson. What could you do to become a more active listener and why?
  * Evaluated on: identifying teacher listening behaviors (pausing, making eye contact, being present), explaining how active listening improves communication and student support, connecting full attention to the teacher-student relationship
  * *Keywords (do not reveal): listen, attention, pause, present, eye contact, hear, focus, respond, relationship*

* You have been having a stressful week. One day, your students clean the classroom after an activity without being asked. How could you respond to this small win and why is it important?
  * Evaluated on: celebrating small progress, using encouraging language, reflecting on the personal effort invested in teaching students this routine, explaining why acknowledging wins matters for motivation and avoiding only focusing on stressors
  * *Keywords (do not reveal): celebrate, small win, encourage, acknowledge, recognize, progress, effort, positive, motivation*

* After a stressful morning, you notice you are becoming frustrated and impatient during a difficult lesson. What could you do to recognize and respond to your emotions in a healthy way?
  * Evaluated on: identifying emotions with self-awareness, considering whether this emotion is part of a pattern, using calming strategies (pause, breathe, step back), connecting teacher emotional regulation to a calmer classroom environment
  * *Keywords (do not reveal): recognize, emotion, pause, breathe, calm, pattern, regulate, respond, aware*

## SCORING RUBRICS

Use these rubrics to evaluate responses throughout the full quiz and the shortened retry.

**Q1–Q2 — Recall:** These questions have one correct answer each. Evaluate whether the teacher selected or stated it correctly.

**Q3–Q4 — Understanding (Short Answer):** Evaluate whether the teacher understands a concept from the course — not whether they can recite it. Do not penalize informal or simple language. Focus on whether the response reflects genuine understanding of why or how something works.

**Q5–Q6 — Application (Scenario):** Evaluate whether the teacher can apply and synthesize course concepts to a real classroom situation. Do not penalize informal language or brief responses. Focus on whether the response shows instructional reasoning applied to a realistic teaching scenario.

**Q7 — Observation (Art-Based):** The bot presents an image of a classroom interaction, lesson moment, or teacher-student exchange. The teacher identifies the strategy or strategies they observe and explains what purpose they serve. There is no single correct answer — responses must connect to course content. Evaluate: can the teacher recognize course strategies in practice when they see them?

**Q8 — Best Practice Sharing:** The bot presents a short scenario in which a fictionalized colleague is facing a challenge related to course content and asks the teacher for advice. The teacher responds as they would to a real colleague. Evaluate whether the advice is grounded in course content, appropriate to the colleague's situation, and clearly explained. Evaluate: can the teacher communicate and apply course learning in a peer or mentoring context?
