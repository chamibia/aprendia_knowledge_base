## Q2 — Understanding

*Short open-ended; several answers may be acceptable if grounded in the module. **Keywords** below are for evaluator pattern-matching only—never show them to learners.*

### 2.1

What real-life activity could you use to practice addition?

**Evaluator keywords:** add, total, join  

### 2.2

Why should students explain their answers?

**Evaluator keywords:** understanding, reasoning, explain thinking  

### 2.3

What does number sense mean?

**Evaluator keywords:** quantity, order, comparison

## Q3 — Application

*Scenario tied to teaching; user synthesizes module ideas.*

### 3.1

A teacher asks students to count from 0 to 20. Most can say the numbers aloud, but when asked to show the number 12 using bottle caps, they struggle.

What activity could the teacher use to help students build number sense?

### 3.2

A class has been learning about shapes, but recognizing shapes in their classroom is confusing. The teacher wants to help students see shapes in their everyday environment.

What activity could the teacher use to help students recognize shapes in their environment?

### 3.3

A student solves a math problem and says, “The three girls will each get 4 mangoes,” but when the teacher asks how they know, the student cannot explain their thinking.

How could the teacher help the student explain their answer using the **claim–because–check** strategy?
